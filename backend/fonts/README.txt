DejaVu Sans is preferred: it includes Turkish glyphs and the lira sign (₺, U+20BA).
Liberation Sans does not contain U+20BA, so it stays as a fallback only.
DejaVu is free to redistribute (Bitstream Vera derivative).
Source: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/
Liberation Sans (SIL Open Font License) remains for hosts without DejaVu.
Source: https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts
